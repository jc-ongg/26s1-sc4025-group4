## Agreed variations from the concept

- One account reserves one seat in new EatTogether Events. Friends need separate accounts. The concept's multi-diner Joining Parties and growing/shrinking Parties therefore do not apply to new Events. Ordinary bookings retain party size; older multi-diner Event records are preserved.
- Public participant cards show exact fictional ages and gender, following the user's choices, rather than age bands. Profile photos are optional; initials are used in the demo.
- Notifications are persisted in-app instead of device push. They can be generated while the server runs and are visible when the app is opened.
- Friendly status labels map `Waiting for diners` to Undersubscribed and `Action needed` to the Cutoff price decision. Event discovery/details use the actual state (including Confirmed and Full price); Bookings uses Upcoming/Past as time categories. Confirmed means the minimum is met, not that the Event is full. Full-price Events never inherit the slot's original discount in the booking panel. Internal Group/API names remain implementation details.
- For you is an additional restaurant-only feature with dining mood and optional typed location. Common food spellings and menu terms constrain local vector ranking. It never fills a weak search with unrelated venues. Past saves/skips do not permanently hide a venue from a new search; shortlisted venues are marked.

## Still deferred

Restaurant accounts/portal, Restaurant Events, Themed Events, merchant opt-in/exclusion controls, Corppass/UEN verification and restaurant attendance/no-show recording remain KIV. Report moderation is a separate administrative gap, not a diner-screen feature; the user explicitly chose to keep it on hold alongside the restaurant portal on 12 October. Mutual Connections, other markets, additional theme filters and a separate EatTogether tab are explicitly roadmap items in the concept.

There is no validated completed-meal North Star dashboard. Scheduled dining time is not attendance evidence. Existing Event timestamps, cutoff releases, Last Call rounds, private feedback and safety records support parts of the proposed measurements, but completed-meal and no-show metrics require verified attendance. Do not claim these metrics or research outcomes were measured merely because their fields exist.
