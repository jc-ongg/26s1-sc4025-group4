Reviewed against the Product Lead's EatTogether concept dated 6 October 2026, updated on 23 October 2026. Restaurant-side demo controls are now approved and implemented. This records actual implementation, not evidence of product validation. The report and editable diagrams remain unchanged for this addition.

## Agreed variations from the concept

- One account reserves one seat in new EatTogether Events. Friends need separate accounts. The concept's multi-diner Joining Parties and growing/shrinking Parties therefore do not apply to new Events. Ordinary bookings retain party size; older multi-diner Event records are preserved.
- Public participant cards show exact fictional ages and gender, following the user's choices, rather than age bands. Profile photos are optional; initials are used in the demo.
- Notifications are persisted in-app instead of device push. They can be generated while the server runs and are visible when the app is opened.
- Friendly status labels map `Waiting for diners` to Undersubscribed and `Action needed` to the Cutoff price decision. Event discovery/details use the actual state (including Confirmed and Full price); Bookings uses Upcoming/Past as time categories. Confirmed means the minimum is met, not that the Event is full. Full-price Events never inherit the slot's original discount in the booking panel. Internal Group/API names remain implementation details.
- For you is an additional restaurant-only feature with dining mood and optional typed location. Common food spellings and menu terms constrain local vector ranking. It never fills a weak search with unrelated venues. Past saves/skips do not permanently hide a venue from a new search; shortlisted venues are marked.

## Still deferred

Interactive mock verification is paused. Real Corppass/UEN and Singpass verification, working registration/recovery, report moderation/appeals and payments remain deferred. Report moderation is a separate administrative gap; the user's 12 October choice to keep it on hold still applies after the restaurant portal's approval. Mutual Connections, other markets, additional theme filters and a separate EatTogether tab remain roadmap items.

There is no validated completed-meal North Star dashboard. Scheduled dining time is not attendance evidence. Restaurant-recorded demo attendance now supplies evidence distinct from the schedule and diner feedback attestation, but no validated KPI dashboard or measured research outcome is implemented. Do not claim metrics were measured merely because their fields exist.

## Restaurant viewpoint and team feedback

- Separate Diner | Restaurant login views in the same app, with working credentials and one account per restaurant. The server enforces account role and venue ownership. Corppass badges are fictional preverified fixtures; registration, recovery and identity sign-in are disabled.
- Reservations, EatTogether and Slots share persisted diner data. Global/slot opt-out affects new Events. Existing allocations protect slot prices and limits.
- Restaurant Events have their own minimum, capacity and discount. They hold the whole configured capacity at creation, release unused seats at Cutoff, and retain the business Host. An empty Event can be withdrawn; joined diners are protected.
- Themed Restaurant Events can set immutable age/gender rules only after stating a theme. Businesses cannot impersonate diners or take over Diner Event Chat.
- The assigned venue records Attended/No-show after booked dining time for its Events. Recording is idempotent and correctable. If excluding no-shows leaves fewer diners than the minimum, full price applies; corrections can restore the agreed discount without overwriting Cutoff full-price acceptance. Attendance is excluded from public participant payloads.
- Restaurant Hosts have confirmed Event Chat and aggregate Feedback for their own Restaurant Events. Individual answers, suggestions and diner reliability histories are not exposed to merchants.
- Diner Event hosts have Leave with Handover explanation; the Cancel Event for everyone button is removed. The older cancellation API remains for compatibility and existing tests.
- Event detail removes repeated remaining-seat/missing-diner counts. Cutoff has a heading, Singapore date/time and a short visible explanation alongside Host's note.
- A shared Recommended menu popup previews the selected slot/Event discount in SGD. These are local illustrative menu fixtures, not live Eatigo pricing or dish ordering.
